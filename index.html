KINGSHOT LITE

Defend the keep. Loot the fallen. Build the towers.

  • Move — WASD / arrows, or drag anywhere to steer
  • Loose — automatic. Your archer draws and fires at the nearest foe
  • Loot — enemies throw coins on death. Walk over them
  • Build — pick a structure from the Build list on the left, then place it anywhere clear
  • Mine — quarries cut stone, the masonry every tower and repair needs
  • Chop — cut trees for wood; wood + stone mends the keep or a battered building

Tip: don't let them reach the keep.
